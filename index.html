ngrok is the most instructive benchmark for Buoyant. Both serve technical audiences, but ngrok leads with the pain ("instead of a hodgepodge of nginx, NLBs, VPNs, model routers, and Cloudflare") before presenting the solution. Buoyant leads with the solution category ("service mesh") before the visitor understands why they need one. The fix isn't simpler content — it's a reorder: pain first, solution second, technology third.
Priority action items for Buoyant
1
Rewrite the hero headline to lead with pain, not product category
Current: "The best service mesh for AWS" — assumes the visitor knows what a service mesh is and has already chosen AWS. Proposed direction: "Zero-trust security and reliability for every Kubernetes app — without changing your code." Lead with the outcome, explain the technology below the fold. ngrok's approach ("instead of nginx + NLBs + VPNs…") is the best model in this comparison set.
2
Move Docs into the main nav
Every competitor has Docs as a first-class nav item. For a product that requires technical evaluation before purchase, Docs is the most important page on the site for developers. Currently it's a secondary button next to "Book a call" — easy to miss and signals the wrong priority to the core audience.
3
Replace "Book a call" as the primary nav CTA with a self-serve path
"Book a call" as the first action gates every non-enterprise visitor to a sales conversation they're not ready for. Adding "Get started free" or "Try Linkerd" as the primary CTA — with "Book a call" as secondary — unlocks the developer-led evaluation funnel. Note: Coder also uses "Get a demo" as its nav CTA but compensates with "Install Coder" as a direct hero path.
4
Add at least one quantitative scale number above the fold
ngrok shows "9M+ developers". Coder's case studies show "90% cost reduction" and "4x faster onboarding". Buoyant has strong logos (Walmart, Xbox) but zero numbers. Even a single real metric — "protecting X billion requests/day" or "trusted by N+ platform teams" — would significantly close the credibility gap with competitors.
5
Make the hero platform-agnostic — move AWS focus to its dedicated landing page
"The best service mesh for AWS" excludes every visitor on GKE, AKS, on-prem, or multi-cloud from the very first line. An AWS-specific page already exists on the site. The homepage should speak to the full addressable audience — AWS can appear as one of several logos or use cases below the fold.
