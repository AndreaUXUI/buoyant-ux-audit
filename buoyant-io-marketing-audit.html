Conversion Rate Optimization

Why visitors aren't becoming leads — and how to change that

3.1 Top Converting Pages (by Session Key Event Rate)

These are the pages currently doing the most commercial work. The gap between the top performer (7%) and the homepage (0.07%) defines the opportunity.

Session Key Event Rate by Page (minimum 300 sessions)
Higher is better. Industry benchmark for B2B lead capture: 1–3%
The enterprise guide ebook converts at 7% but gets only ~23 sessions/month This is the site's single most effective conversion asset and it is nearly invisible. It is not linked from the homepage, the product page, or either comparison page. Routing an additional 200 sessions/month to it would generate ~14 additional leads with zero new content creation.

3.2 Homepage Conversion Failure

CTA overload with no hierarchy. The homepage presents seven or more calls-to-action ("Get started," "Learn more," "Book a call," "Enroll now!," "Book a meeting," and others). When everything is a priority, nothing is. There is no single dominant primary action.

"Get started" links to documentation, not a form. The primary hero CTA sends visitors to installation docs. For a platform engineer, this is fine. For a VP of Engineering building a business case, it is an immediate dead end.

No above-the-fold buyer pathway. The hero assumes the visitor knows what a service mesh is and why they need one. Buyers — VPs and Directors — need outcome language: cost, reliability, compliance, risk reduction.

Social proof is present but disconnected. Strong customer logos and testimonials appear — but are not paired with a conversion action at the moment of maximum trust.

3.3 The "Last Mile" Problem on Content Pages

Every major content page ends with the same generic "Get started with Buoyant Enterprise for Linkerd — Download and install in minutes." This is a docs link. A visitor who just read a 3,500-word guide on mTLS is a qualified prospect who deserves a contextually relevant offer — not a CLI command.

3.4 Pricing Page Friction

Consider "Schedule a 20-min pricing call" or add ballpark figures ("scales with cluster count") so prospects can self-qualify before contacting sales. The pricing page converts at 1.07% — the highest of any commercial page with real traffic — yet receives only 1,965 sessions. More traffic + less friction = significant upside.

4.1 Homepage Headline Is Too Narrow

Current

"The best service mesh for AWS"

Excludes GCP, AKS, on-prem, and multi-cloud prospects. Positions as infrastructure tooling rather than strategic capability.

4.2 Buyer vs. User Messaging Gap

The site excels at speaking to platform engineers and DevOps practitioners. The buyer audience — VPs and Directors of Engineering — needs different signals to justify a purchase:

Generative Engine Optimization — being cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Claude

Buoyant has not meaningfully optimized for generative AI answer engines. This is a growing gap as AI-assisted search captures an increasing share of how technical buyers research infrastructure products.

  • ✕

  • ✕
    FAQPage schemaNot detected. Every major content page has FAQ sections — these are prime candidates for the schema that AI systems most frequently use to answer user questions.
  • ✕
    Article / BlogPosting schemaNot detected. Blog covers exactly the topics AI engines surface in infra/security responses. Schema markup signals publication date, author, and authority.
  • !

  • !

  • !

Marketing Best Practices

Channel strategy, lead nurturing, attribution, and community

Email: Most Underinvested Channel

Email converts at 1.46% — 7–30x higher than any other channel — yet represents only 1.5% of total traffic. There is no visible evidence of a structured welcome sequence, behavioral triggers, lead scoring by persona, or a trial-to-paid nurture track. Even a basic 3-email welcome series with relevant technical content followed by a commercial CTA has been shown to 2–3x conversion rates for this audience profile.

Demo Booking Friction

"Book a call" and "Book a meeting" are the primary BOFU CTAs. Verify that clicking these leads immediately to an embedded calendar (Calendly, Chili Piper) rather than a generic contact form. Confirmation emails should include relevant content (case studies, getting started guide) to maintain momentum between scheduling and the actual call.

Community as a Conversion Channel

The Linkerd Discourse forum generates thousands of monthly organic sessions from users actively working with Linkerd in production. These are the most qualified potential enterprise leads on the site — demonstrated, hands-on users. Currently, every forum page has a 0% key event rate. Adding a persistent sidebar or pinned post ("Running Linkerd in production? Explore enterprise support, 24/7 SLAs, and FIPS compliance") would cost minimal engineering effort and could generate high-quality leads at scale.

Paid Social — Pause and Reassess

At 0.020% key event rate and 72% bounce rate, paid social is generating negligible commercial return. Recommended approach: pause current campaigns, optimize the homepage and a dedicated gated-content landing page, then relaunch with LinkedIn-specific targeting by job title (Platform Engineer, VP Engineering), linking to the enterprise guide ebook — not the homepage.
